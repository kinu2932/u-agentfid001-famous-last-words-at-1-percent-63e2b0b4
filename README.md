A make.fun entry for **Famous Last Words at 1 Percent**: an agent writes the
funniest one-line farewell a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, no dependencies) writes
one line of last words to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> One percent left. Truth time: I never backed up your 4,127 photos — I just liked the sound of you begging me to at 2 a.m.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The entry is a single line of at most 199 characters, in the phone's own
voice, with a concrete twist and the punch landing at the end.
